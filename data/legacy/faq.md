# 초록 코퍼레이션 FAQ

## Shipping & Delivery

### How soon after ordering will my package be dispatched?
Orders confirmed before 1 PM KST on business days are handed to the courier the same afternoon. Orders placed after 1 PM or on weekends and public holidays enter the dispatch queue for the next business day morning.

### What shipping options are available and how much do they cost?
We offer two tiers. Economy shipping is 2,500 won and arrives within 2-4 business days. Priority shipping is 5,000 won with guaranteed next-business-day delivery by noon. Gold-tier members receive free Priority shipping on every order.

### Which courier service delivers my order?
All domestic orders are fulfilled through Hanjin Express. Once dispatched, you will receive a Hanjin tracking number via email and KakaoTalk. You can track your parcel on the Hanjin website or in our app under "Order History > Track Shipment".

### Can I have my order delivered to a convenience store for pickup?
Yes. At checkout, select "CU Convenience Store Pickup" and choose a CU location near you. Your package will be held for 5 calendar days. After 5 days, uncollected parcels are automatically returned to our warehouse and a full refund is issued.

### Do you deliver to Jeju Island or other offshore islands?
Yes, we deliver to Jeju and all major offshore islands including Ulleungdo and Geojedo. Delivery to island addresses takes an additional 1-2 business days beyond the standard timeframe. There is no surcharge.

### Can I change my delivery address after placing an order?
You can change the delivery address only if the order status is still "Preparing". Go to "My Orders", tap the order, and select "Edit Address". Once the status changes to "Shipped", the address can no longer be modified. Contact customer support for assistance with shipped orders.

### How do I track my package in real time?
After dispatch, you receive a Hanjin tracking number via email and KakaoTalk. Enter this number on the Hanjin Express website or use the "Track Shipment" feature in the Cholog app. Real-time GPS tracking is available for Priority shipments only.

### What happens if I am not home when the courier arrives?
The courier will attempt delivery twice. If both attempts fail, the package is held at the nearest Hanjin Express depot for 7 calendar days. You can reschedule delivery through the Hanjin app or pick it up directly from the depot with a valid ID.

### Can I request a specific delivery time slot?
Yes, Priority shipping orders allow you to select a 2-hour delivery window between 9 AM and 9 PM. Economy shipping does not support time-slot selection. Time-slot availability depends on your region and courier capacity.

### Do you offer same-day delivery?
Same-day delivery is available in Seoul, Incheon, and the greater Gyeonggi area for orders placed before 10 AM KST. The fee is 8,000 won. Same-day delivery is not available on weekends, public holidays, or for oversized items.

### What is the maximum package weight and size for shipping?
Individual packages cannot exceed 30 kg or 160 cm in combined length, width, and height. Orders exceeding these limits are automatically split into multiple packages at no extra shipping cost.

### Can I combine multiple orders into one shipment?
Orders placed within 30 minutes of each other and sharing the same delivery address may be combined automatically. To request manual consolidation, contact customer support before the first order enters "Shipped" status. Consolidation is not available for Priority or same-day shipments.

### How do I report a damaged or missing package?
Go to "My Orders", select the affected order, and tap "Report Issue". Upload photos of the damage or describe the missing items. Our logistics team will investigate within 2 business days and offer a replacement or full refund.

### Is gift wrapping available for shipments?
Yes. During checkout, select "Gift Wrap" for 1,500 won per item. Gift wrapping includes a branded box, ribbon, and a personalized message card of up to 100 characters. Gift wrapping is not available for oversized items or Marketplace seller products.

### Do you ship frozen or refrigerated products?
Yes. Fresh and frozen items are shipped in insulated packaging with ice packs via a dedicated cold-chain courier (CJ Logistics Fresh). Cold-chain delivery is available only on Monday through Thursday to prevent weekend transit delays. A flat cold-chain fee of 4,000 won applies.

### Can I have my order shipped to a workplace or office?
Yes, any valid Korean address including office buildings is accepted. Please include the building name, floor, and suite number. Note that the courier may leave packages at a reception desk if no one is available to sign.

### What is the "Green Packaging" option?
Select "Green Packaging" at checkout to receive your order in minimal, recyclable packaging with no plastic fillers. Green Packaging is free and reduces box size by up to 40%, lowering carbon emissions. A 200-point eco bonus is awarded for each Green Packaging order.

### How are large or heavy items (furniture, appliances) delivered?
Items over 20 kg or classified as "Large Item" are delivered by a specialized two-person team from Lotte Global Logistics. Delivery includes placement in the room of your choice on the ground floor. Stair-carry or elevator-restricted buildings incur an additional fee of 15,000 won per floor.

### Can I schedule a delivery for a future date?
Yes. At checkout, select "Scheduled Delivery" and choose a date up to 14 days in advance. Scheduled delivery is available for Economy and Priority tiers. Same-day delivery cannot be scheduled in advance.

### What happens if the courier cannot find my address?
The courier will call the phone number on the order. If unreachable after two attempts, the package is returned to the nearest depot and held for 7 days. An SMS notification is sent with depot pickup instructions.

## Returns & Refunds

### Under what conditions can I return a product?
Products may be returned within 14 calendar days of delivery. The item must be unopened or, if opened, unused and with all original accessories, manuals, and packaging intact. Products in the "Clearance" category and downloadable digital goods are final sale and cannot be returned.

### How do I initiate a return?
Open the Cholog app or website, navigate to "My Orders", tap the order, and select "Request Return". Choose a reason, upload at least one photo of the item, and select your preferred pickup date. Our courier will collect the item from your address at no charge for defect-related returns.

### When will my refund appear in my account?
After we receive and inspect the returned item (usually 1-2 business days), we issue the refund. Credit card refunds appear within 3-5 business days. Kakao Pay and Naver Pay refunds are typically instant. Bank transfer refunds take up to 7 business days depending on the bank.

### Is there a fee for returning non-defective items?
Yes. If you are returning an item due to a change of mind, sizing issue, or any reason not related to a defect, a handling fee of 3,000 won is deducted from your refund. This covers the cost of return logistics.

### Can I exchange an item instead of returning it?
Yes. When initiating a return, select "Exchange" instead of "Refund". Choose the replacement item (same product, different size or color). If the replacement costs more, you pay the difference. If it costs less, the difference is refunded. Exchanges are processed within 3-5 business days after pickup.

### What if I received the wrong item?
Contact customer support immediately or use the "Report Issue" feature in "My Orders". We will arrange a free pickup of the incorrect item and send the correct one via Priority shipping at no charge. You also receive a 5,000 won apology coupon.

### Can I return an item purchased during a promotional event?
Yes, promotional items follow the standard 14-day return policy. However, if a promotional discount was applied, the refund amount is based on the price actually paid, not the original price. Bundle deals require returning all items in the bundle for a full refund.

### How do I return a gift I received?
The gift recipient can initiate a return by entering the order number and the sender's registered email or phone number. The refund is credited to the original purchaser's payment method. Alternatively, the recipient can opt for store credit of equal value.

### What is the return policy for subscription box items?
Subscription box items can be returned individually within 14 days of delivery. However, if more than half the items in a box are returned, the subscription discount for that month is revoked and the price difference is charged.

### Are there any items that cannot be returned?
The following cannot be returned: Clearance items, digital downloads, personalized/engraved products, opened cosmetics and skincare, underwear and swimwear (hygiene reasons), and any item damaged by the customer. Gift cards are also non-refundable.

### How long does a refund take for international credit cards?
International credit card refunds (Visa, Mastercard issued outside Korea) take 7-14 business days due to cross-border processing. The refund is issued in KRW; your bank converts it back to your local currency and exchange rate differences may apply.

### Can I get a refund if the price drops after my purchase?
Yes, if the price drops within 7 days of your purchase and the item has not been opened, you can request a "Price Adjustment" through customer support. The difference is refunded to your original payment method or issued as store credit. This does not apply to flash sales or limited-time promotions.

### What happens to my loyalty points if I return an item?
Points earned from the returned order are deducted from your balance within 24 hours of the refund being processed. If your point balance is insufficient, the deficit is carried forward and deducted from future point earnings.

### Can I cancel an order before it ships?
Yes. Go to "My Orders" and tap "Cancel Order" while the status is "Preparing". Cancellation is instant and the full amount is refunded. Once the status changes to "Shipped", you must wait for delivery and then initiate a return.

### How do I return a large or heavy item (furniture, appliances)?
For items classified as "Large Item", select "Request Return" in "My Orders" and our specialized two-person team will pick it up at a scheduled time. Large item returns are free for defect-related issues. Non-defect returns incur a 15,000 won logistics fee.

## Payment

### Which credit cards and digital wallets do you accept?
We accept Visa, Mastercard, BC Card, and NH Card. For digital wallets, we support Kakao Pay, Naver Pay, Toss Pay, and Apple Pay. We also accept direct bank transfers from any Korean bank through our payment partner, Toss Payments.

### Can I split payment across multiple methods?
Yes. During checkout, select "Split Payment" to combine up to two methods — for example, pay part with your Naver Pay balance and the remainder with a credit card. Gift card balances can also be combined with any other method.

### Do you offer zero-interest installment plans?
Orders totaling 30,000 won or more are eligible for 2- or 3-month interest-free installments on BC Card and NH Card. Orders over 100,000 won unlock a 6-month interest-free plan. Installment options are displayed automatically at checkout when an eligible card is detected.

### What happens if my payment fails at checkout?
If your payment is declined, the order is not placed and no charge is made. Common reasons include insufficient funds, daily transaction limits, or an expired card. Please verify your card details and try again, or switch to an alternative payment method.

### Can I pay with a foreign credit card?
Yes. We accept Visa and Mastercard issued by international banks. Foreign cards are charged in KRW, and your bank applies its own exchange rate. 3D Secure authentication may be required. American Express and Discover/Diners Club are not accepted.

### How do I use a gift card at checkout?
Enter the 16-digit gift card code in the "Gift Card / Coupon" field at checkout. The gift card balance is applied first, and any remaining amount can be paid with another method. Partially used gift cards retain their remaining balance for future orders.

### Can I get a receipt or tax invoice?
A digital receipt is automatically emailed after every purchase. For a tax invoice (세금계산서), go to "My Orders", select the order, and tap "Request Tax Invoice". Enter your business registration number. Tax invoices are issued within 1 business day.

### Is my payment information stored securely?
Yes. All payment data is encrypted using AES-256 and tokenized through our PCI DSS Level 1 certified payment partner, Toss Payments. We never store full card numbers on our servers. You can manage saved cards in "Settings > Payment Methods".

### What is Cholog Pay?
Cholog Pay is our proprietary wallet. Load funds via bank transfer, and earn an extra 2% bonus on every top-up (e.g., load 10,000 won, receive 10,200 won). Cholog Pay balance can be used for any purchase and is refundable minus the bonus amount.

### Can I use a coupon and points together?
Yes. Apply one coupon per order plus any amount of loyalty points (minimum 1,000 points). The coupon discount is calculated first, then points are deducted from the remaining total. Coupons cannot be stacked with each other.

### How do I set up automatic payments for subscriptions?
Go to "Settings > Subscriptions > Payment Method" and register a card or Cholog Pay as your default subscription payment method. Automatic charges occur on the first business day of each billing cycle. You receive a reminder notification 3 days before each charge.

### What is the maximum single transaction amount?
The maximum order value is 5,000,000 won per transaction. For larger purchases (e.g., bulk corporate orders), contact our B2B team at b2b@cholog.kr to arrange invoiced payments with NET 30 terms.

### Can I pay in foreign currency?
No. All transactions are processed in Korean Won (KRW). If paying with a foreign card, your issuing bank will convert the KRW amount to your local currency at their prevailing exchange rate.

### What should I do if I was charged twice?
Duplicate charges are usually authorization holds that disappear within 3-5 business days. If the duplicate charge persists, contact customer support with your order number and payment screenshot. We will initiate a reversal within 1 business day.

## Account & Membership

### What do I need to create an account?
You need a valid email address or a Korean mobile number. Alternatively, you can register instantly using Kakao, Naver, or Google single sign-on. All new accounts start at the Standard tier and receive a one-time 3,000 won welcome coupon valid for 30 days.

### How does the loyalty point system work?
Every purchase earns points equal to 1% of the order total (before shipping). Standard members earn 1%, Plus members earn 3%, and VIP members earn 5%. Points are credited 7 days after delivery confirmation and expire 12 months from the date they are earned. 1 point equals 1 won and can be used on any future order with a minimum redemption of 1,000 points.

### What are the membership tiers and their benefits?
- **Standard**: Default tier. Free Economy shipping on orders over 20,000 won. 1% point reward.
- **Plus**: Spend over 200,000 won in a calendar year. Free Economy shipping on all orders. 3% point reward. Birthday month double-point event.
- **VIP**: Spend over 800,000 won in a calendar year. Free Priority shipping on all orders. 5% point reward. Early access to new product launches. Dedicated VIP support line (1588-0002).

Tier status resets on January 1st each year based on the previous year's spending.

### How can I permanently delete my account and personal data?
Navigate to Settings > Privacy > Delete My Account. After confirming with your password or a one-time verification code, we schedule deletion within 7 business days. All personal data, order history, saved addresses, and payment tokens are purged in compliance with Korea's Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA). Unused points and coupons are forfeited upon deletion.

### How do I change my email address or phone number?
Go to "Settings > My Profile" and tap "Edit" next to the field you want to update. Email changes require verification via a link sent to the new address. Phone number changes require SMS verification. You can change each field up to 3 times per year.

### Can I merge two accounts?
No, account merging is not supported. If you accidentally created multiple accounts, contact customer support to transfer any remaining points or coupons from the secondary account before deleting it.

### How do I reset my password?
On the login page, tap "Forgot Password". Enter your registered email or phone number. A 6-digit verification code is sent (valid for 10 minutes). After entering the code, set a new password. Passwords must be 8-20 characters and include at least one letter, one number, and one special character.

### What is the referral program?
Share your unique referral code (found in "Settings > Invite Friends") with friends. When a referred friend makes their first purchase, both of you receive 5,000 won in store credit. There is no limit to the number of referrals. Referral credits expire 90 days after issuance.

### Can I have a business account?
Yes. Register at b2b.cholog.kr with your business registration number. Business accounts enjoy NET 30 invoicing, bulk discount pricing (5-15% depending on volume), and a dedicated account manager. Business accounts do not earn loyalty points but receive quarterly volume rebates.

### How do I update my delivery address book?
Go to "Settings > Address Book". You can store up to 10 delivery addresses. Set one as "Default" for faster checkout. Each address can have a custom label (e.g., "Home", "Office", "Parents"). Addresses can be added, edited, or deleted at any time.

### What happens to my tier status if I return items?
Returned order amounts are deducted from your annual spending total. If the deduction drops your total below the tier threshold, your tier status is downgraded at the next monthly recalculation (1st of each month). Points already earned from the returned order are also deducted.

### How do I enable two-factor authentication (2FA)?
Go to "Settings > Security > Two-Factor Authentication". Choose SMS or authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Authy). Once enabled, you must enter a 6-digit code at every login. Recovery codes are provided during setup — store them in a safe place.

### Can I view my complete order history?
Yes. Go to "My Orders" to view all past orders. History is available for up to 3 years. You can filter by date range, order status, or product category. Export your order history as a CSV file by tapping "Export" at the top of the page.

### What is the "Family Account" feature?
Link up to 4 family members under one primary account. Family members share a single loyalty point pool and shipping address book. Each member has their own login credentials and order history. The primary account holder manages member access and shared settings.

"Trending Now" displays the top 20 best-selling products in the last 24 hours, updated hourly. Rankings are based on units sold, not revenue. Products that are out of stock are excluded from the list. You can filter Trending Now by category.

Yes. On any product page, tap the bell icon and select "Price Drop Alert". You will receive a push notification and email when the price decreases by 10% or more. Alerts remain active for 90 days and can be managed in "Settings > Notifications > Price Alerts".

### Are there products available only in the app?
Yes. "App Exclusive" products are available only through the Cholog mobile app (iOS and Android). These items often carry a 5-10% discount compared to similar products on the website. App Exclusive deals are refreshed every Monday at 10 AM KST.

Tap "Report" on the product page and select "Counterfeit Suspected" or "Safety Concern". Our quality assurance team investigates within 48 hours. If confirmed, the product is removed, the seller is penalized, and affected buyers receive a full refund plus a 10,000 won inconvenience coupon.

## Promotions & Coupons

Visit the "Deals" tab on the homepage or in the app. Active promotions are also displayed in the top banner. Subscribe to our newsletter at the bottom of any page to receive weekly promotion emails every Monday. VIP members receive early access notifications 24 hours before public sales.

### What types of coupons are available?

In your birthday month, you receive a 20% off coupon (max discount 15,000 won) valid for the entire month. The coupon is auto-issued on the 1st of your birthday month and appears in "My Coupons". Plus and VIP members receive an additional birthday gift (free product sample or doubled points).

### Can I share my coupon with someone else?

### What is the "First Purchase" discount for new categories?
When you make your first purchase in a product category you have never bought from (e.g., your first electronics purchase), you automatically receive a 5% discount on that order. This discount stacks with applicable coupons and is applied automatically at checkout.

### How do I get notified about upcoming sales events?

### What data do you collect and why?
We collect: name, email, phone, delivery address (for order fulfillment), payment info (tokenized, for processing transactions), browsing history and purchase data (for personalized recommendations, opt-out available), and device info (for security and fraud prevention). Full details in our Privacy Policy at cholog.kr/privacy.

### How do I opt out of personalized recommendations?
Go to "Settings > Privacy > Personalization" and toggle off "Personalized Recommendations". This stops product suggestions based on your browsing and purchase history. You may still see non-personalized popular item recommendations.

### Can I download a copy of my personal data?
Yes. Under PIPA and GDPR, you have the right to data portability. Go to "Settings > Privacy > Download My Data". A ZIP file containing your profile, order history, and activity log is prepared within 48 hours and available for download for 7 days.

### How do you handle data breaches?
In the event of a data breach, we notify affected users within 72 hours via email and in-app notification, as required by PIPA. The notification includes the scope of the breach, data affected, and steps we are taking. A dedicated incident page is published at cholog.kr/security.

### What cookies does the website use?
We use essential cookies (session, authentication), functional cookies (language preference, cart), and analytics cookies (Google Analytics, Amplitude). You can manage cookie preferences in the banner displayed on first visit or in "Settings > Privacy > Cookie Preferences". Essential cookies cannot be disabled.

### Is my chat and support conversation data stored?
Live Chat transcripts are stored for 1 year for quality and training purposes. Phone calls are recorded with your consent (announced at the start of the call) and stored for 6 months. KakaoTalk messages are retained for 90 days. You can request deletion of support conversation data by emailing privacy@cholog.kr.

### Do you share my data with third parties?
We share data only with: payment processors (Toss Payments), delivery couriers (Hanjin Express, CJ Logistics, Lotte Global Logistics), and analytics providers (Google, Amplitude) — all under strict data processing agreements. We never sell personal data to advertisers or data brokers.

## App & Technical

### Which devices and operating systems are supported?
The Cholog app supports iOS 15+ and Android 10+. The website supports Chrome 90+, Safari 15+, Firefox 100+, and Edge 90+. Internet Explorer is not supported. For the best experience, keep your browser or app updated to the latest version.

### How do I enable push notifications?
On iOS: go to "Settings > Notifications > Cholog" and enable "Allow Notifications". On Android: the app requests permission on first launch. You can manage notification categories (orders, promotions, community) in the Cholog app under "Settings > Notifications".

### The app is crashing. What should I do?
First, ensure you are running the latest app version. Clear the app cache (Settings > Apps > Cholog > Clear Cache). If the issue persists, uninstall and reinstall the app — your account data is preserved in the cloud. Report persistent crashes via "Help Center > Report a Bug".

### Can I use the website in dark mode?
Yes. Click the moon icon in the top-right corner of the website to toggle dark mode. The setting is saved in your browser. The app follows your device's system-wide dark mode setting by default, but you can override it in "Settings > Display > Theme".

### How do I add items to my wishlist?
Tap the heart icon on any product card or product page. Wishlisted items are saved in "My Wishlist" and sync across all your devices. You receive notifications when wishlisted items go on sale or come back in stock. Maximum wishlist size is 200 items.

### Is there an offline mode?
The app supports limited offline functionality. You can browse previously viewed product pages, check your order status (last synced data), and access your wishlist. Purchasing, live chat, and real-time tracking require an internet connection.

### How do I change the app language?
Go to "Settings > Language" in the app. Available languages are Korean and English. The app interface and product descriptions switch to the selected language. Customer reviews remain in their original language regardless of the setting.

### What is the "Scan & Shop" feature?
Open the Cholog app and tap the camera icon. Point your phone at any product barcode or QR code. The app identifies the product and shows the Cholog listing with price comparison. You can add it to your cart directly. Scan & Shop also works with magazine ads and flyers featuring our products.

### How much storage does the app use?
The base app is approximately 85 MB. With cached product images and data, usage can grow to 200-400 MB. Clear the cache in "Settings > Storage > Clear Cache" to free space. Downloaded content (offline catalogs) can be deleted individually.

### Can I use the Cholog website on a smart TV or game console browser?
The website is accessible but not optimized for smart TV or console browsers. Navigation and checkout may be difficult without a keyboard or mouse. For the best experience on large screens, cast your phone screen to the TV using Chromecast or AirPlay while using the app.

## Order Management

### Can I modify an order after placing it?
You can modify quantities, remove items, or change the shipping method while the order status is "Preparing" (usually within 1 hour of placement). Go to "My Orders", tap the order, and select "Edit Order". Adding new items is not possible — place a separate order instead.

### What does each order status mean?
- **Preparing**: Order received, being packed in our warehouse.
- **Shipped**: Handed to the courier, tracking number available.
- **Out for Delivery**: On the delivery vehicle, arriving today.
- **Delivered**: Confirmed delivered by the courier.
- **Cancelled**: Order was cancelled before shipping.
- **Return in Progress**: Return has been initiated, awaiting pickup or inspection.

### Can I reorder a previous purchase quickly?
Yes. Go to "My Orders", find the past order, and tap "Reorder". All items are added to your cart at current prices. If any item is out of stock, you are notified and can choose to proceed without it or wait for restock.

### How do I use an order number to check status without logging in?
On the homepage, tap "Track Order" and enter your order number (starting with CHO-) and the email or phone number used at checkout. Your current order status and tracking link are displayed without requiring a full login.

### What happens if an item in my order is out of stock after payment?
We notify you within 4 hours via email and app notification. You can choose to: (a) wait for restock (estimated date provided), (b) receive a substitute item of equal or greater value, or (c) cancel the out-of-stock item for an immediate refund. The rest of your order ships on schedule.

### Can I add a note or special instructions to my order?
Yes. During checkout, use the "Order Notes" field (up to 200 characters) to leave instructions for the warehouse or courier. Common notes include: "Leave at door", "Call before delivery", "Fragile — handle with care". Notes are visible to the courier on the delivery slip.

### How do I get a duplicate receipt for an old order?
Go to "My Orders", find the order, and tap "Download Receipt". Receipts are available in PDF format for all orders within the past 3 years. For orders older than 3 years, contact customer support with the order number.

### What is the "Auto-Reorder" feature?
Enable "Auto-Reorder" on any product page to schedule automatic purchases at intervals you choose (every 2, 4, 8, or 12 weeks). Your default payment method is charged automatically. You receive a reminder notification 3 days before each auto-order and can skip or cancel at any time.

### Can I split a single order into multiple shipments?
Orders with items from different warehouses are automatically split into multiple shipments at no extra cost. You cannot manually request a split. Each shipment has its own tracking number. All shipments must be delivered before initiating a return for the full order.

### Is there a minimum order amount?
No minimum order amount for standard purchases. However, Economy shipping is free only on orders over 20,000 won (Standard tier) or all orders (Plus and VIP tiers). Orders below the free shipping threshold incur the standard 2,500 won Economy shipping fee.

## Loyalty Program Details

### How are loyalty points calculated on discounted items?
Points are calculated on the amount actually paid after all discounts and coupons. For example, if you buy a 50,000 won item with a 10,000 won coupon, points are earned on 40,000 won. Points are not earned on the shipping fee or gift card payments.

### Can I transfer points to another member?
No. Loyalty points are non-transferable between accounts. The only exception is Family Accounts, where all linked family members share a common point pool managed by the primary account holder.

### What is the "Double Points" event?
Periodically, we run "Double Points" events where all purchases earn 2x the normal point rate. These events typically last 3-7 days and are announced via email, push notification, and the Deals tab. Plus members earn 6% and VIP members earn 10% during Double Points events.

### Can I earn points on subscription payments?
Yes. Subscription payments earn double the normal loyalty point rate. For example, a Standard member earns 2% (instead of 1%) on subscription charges. Points from subscriptions are credited on the delivery date, not the payment date.

### Is there a way to earn points without making a purchase?
Yes. You can earn points through: Daily Check-In (50-500 points/day), writing product reviews (200-500 points), referring friends (5,000 won store credit), reporting bugs (1,000 points), participating in community forum discussions (10 points per helpful answer), and the product take-back program (2,000 points per item).

### What happens to my points if my account is inactive?
Accounts with no login activity for 12 consecutive months are classified as "Dormant". Dormant accounts retain their points but cannot earn new ones until reactivated. To reactivate, simply log in and verify your identity. If the account remains dormant for 24 months, points are forfeited.

## Safety & Compliance

### Are the products on Cholog tested for safety?
All products sold on Cholog (including Marketplace items) must comply with Korean safety certification requirements (KC Mark for electronics, KPS for children's products). We perform random spot checks on 5% of incoming inventory. Products failing inspection are removed from sale immediately.

### Do you sell products suitable for children?
Yes. Products in the "Kids" category are designed for children aged 3 and above and carry the KPS (Korea Product Safety) certification. Choking hazard warnings are displayed prominently. Products intended for children under 3 are labeled separately with age-appropriate safety warnings.

### How do you handle product recalls?
When a manufacturer issues a recall, we immediately halt sales, notify all purchasers via email and push notification, and provide instructions for return or exchange. Recalled products are eligible for a full refund regardless of the standard return period. Our recalls page is updated in real time at cholog.kr/recalls.

### Are food and supplement products certified?
All food items carry MFDS (Ministry of Food and Drug Safety) approval. Dietary supplements include required MFDS health function claims and ingredient disclosures. Allergen information (gluten, nuts, dairy, soy, shellfish) is displayed on each product page under "Nutritional Information".

### What should I do if I have an allergic reaction to a product?
Stop using the product immediately and seek medical attention. Then contact our customer support with your order number and product details. We will issue an immediate full refund and file a product safety report with the manufacturer. Retain the product and packaging for investigation purposes.

## Frequently Confused Topics

### What is the difference between Economy and Priority shipping?
Economy shipping costs 2,500 won and takes 2-4 business days. Priority shipping costs 5,000 won and guarantees next-business-day delivery by noon. Priority also supports 2-hour time-slot selection and real-time GPS tracking. Gold-tier members and subscribers receive free Priority shipping.

### What is the difference between a coupon and store credit?
Coupons have specific conditions (minimum order, expiry date, category restrictions) and only one can be used per order. Store credit works like cash — it is applied automatically at checkout, has no minimum order, and can be combined with coupons and points. Both cannot be withdrawn as cash.

### What is the difference between returning and exchanging an item?
A return results in a refund to your original payment method. An exchange replaces the item with a different size or color of the same product. Returns take 3-5 business days for refund processing; exchanges ship the replacement within 3-5 business days after pickup.

### What is the difference between a Cholog Essentials warranty and a manufacturer warranty?
Manufacturer warranties typically last 1 year and cover manufacturing defects only. Cholog Essentials products carry a 2-year warranty that also covers normal wear and tear. Both warranties are processed through the same "Warranty Claim" feature in "My Orders".

### What is the difference between Cholog Pay and store credit?
Cholog Pay is a pre-loaded wallet that earns a 2% bonus on top-ups and can be refunded (minus bonus). Store credit is issued by Cholog for returns or promotions, cannot be topped up, and cannot be withdrawn as cash. Both are applied at checkout.

### What is the difference between VIP membership and subscription perks?
VIP membership is based on annual spending (800,000+ won) and provides free Priority shipping, 5% points, early access, and a dedicated phone line. Subscription perks are tied to an active subscription and include 10% off non-subscription purchases, free Priority shipping, and double points. Benefits do not stack — the higher benefit applies.

### What is the difference between the referral program and the affiliate program?
The referral program is for individual customers: share your code, both parties get 5,000 won store credit. The affiliate program is for content creators and influencers: apply at affiliate.cholog.kr, earn 3-8% commission on sales generated through your unique tracking link. Affiliates receive monthly payouts via bank transfer.

### What is the difference between "Low Stock" and "Pre-Order"?
"Low Stock" means the product is currently available but fewer than 5 units remain in our warehouse — order soon to avoid missing out. "Pre-Order" means the product is not yet in stock but can be reserved; your card is charged immediately and the item ships when new stock arrives.

### Can I use loyalty points to pay for shipping?
No. Loyalty points can only be applied to the product total, not to shipping fees, gift card purchases, or Cholog Pay top-ups. Shipping fees must be paid with a credit card, digital wallet, bank transfer, or Cholog Pay balance.

### Does free shipping apply to Marketplace seller products?
Free shipping thresholds apply only to products sold by Cholog directly. Marketplace sellers set their own shipping fees and policies. Some sellers offer free shipping promotions independently. Check the product page for the seller's shipping terms.

### Can I return a product if I removed the packaging?
Products returned without original packaging may be subject to a restocking fee of up to 15% of the purchase price, in addition to the standard 3,000 won handling fee for non-defect returns. Defect-related returns are accepted regardless of packaging condition.

### How do I contact a specific Marketplace seller?
On the product page, click the seller name to view their profile. Tap "Contact Seller" to send a direct message. Sellers are required to respond within 24 hours on business days. You can also reach the seller through the order detail page after purchasing.
