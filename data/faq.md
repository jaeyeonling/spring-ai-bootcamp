## Shipping & Delivery

### How soon after ordering will my package be dispatched?
Orders confirmed before 1 PM KST on business days are handed to the courier the same afternoon. Orders placed after 1 PM or on weekends and public holidays enter the dispatch queue for the next business day morning.

### What shipping options are available and how much do they cost?
We offer two tiers. Economy shipping is 2,500 won and arrives within 2-4 business days. Priority shipping is 5,000 won with guaranteed next-business-day delivery by noon. Gold-tier members receive free Priority shipping on every order.

### Which courier service delivers my order?
All domestic orders are fulfilled through Hanjin Express. Once dispatched, you will receive a Hanjin tracking number via email and KakaoTalk. You can track your parcel on the Hanjin website or in our app under "Order History > Track Shipment".

### Can I have my order delivered to a convenience store for pickup?
Yes. At checkout, select "CU Convenience Store Pickup" and choose a CU location near you. Your package will be held for 5 calendar days. After 5 days, uncollected parcels are automatically returned to our warehouse and a full refund is issued.

### Do you deliver to Jeju Island or other offshore islands?
Yes, we deliver to Jeju and all major offshore islands including Ulleungdo and Geojedo. Delivery to island addresses takes an additional 1-2 business days beyond the standard timeframe. There is no surcharge.

## Returns & Refunds

### Under what conditions can I return a product?
Products may be returned within 14 calendar days of delivery. The item must be unopened or, if opened, unused and with all original accessories, manuals, and packaging intact. Products in the "Clearance" category and downloadable digital goods are final sale and cannot be returned.

### How do I initiate a return?
Open the Cholog app or website, navigate to "My Orders", tap the order, and select "Request Return". Choose a reason, upload at least one photo of the item, and select your preferred pickup date. Our courier will collect the item from your address at no charge for defect-related returns.

### When will my refund appear in my account?
After we receive and inspect the returned item (usually 1-2 business days), we issue the refund. Credit card refunds appear within 3-5 business days. Kakao Pay and Naver Pay refunds are typically instant. Bank transfer refunds take up to 7 business days depending on the bank.

### Is there a fee for returning non-defective items?
Yes. If you are returning an item due to a change of mind, sizing issue, or any reason not related to a defect, a handling fee of 3,000 won is deducted from your refund. This covers the cost of return logistics.

## Payment

### Which credit cards and digital wallets do you accept?
We accept Visa, Mastercard, BC Card, and NH Card. For digital wallets, we support Kakao Pay, Naver Pay, Toss Pay, and Apple Pay. We also accept direct bank transfers from any Korean bank through our payment partner, Toss Payments.

### Can I split payment across multiple methods?
Yes. During checkout, select "Split Payment" to combine up to two methods — for example, pay part with your Naver Pay balance and the remainder with a credit card. Gift card balances can also be combined with any other method.

### Do you offer zero-interest installment plans?
Orders totaling 30,000 won or more are eligible for 2- or 3-month interest-free installments on BC Card and NH Card. Orders over 100,000 won unlock a 6-month interest-free plan. Installment options are displayed automatically at checkout when an eligible card is detected.

### What happens if my payment fails at checkout?
If your payment is declined, the order is not placed and no charge is made. Common reasons include insufficient funds, daily transaction limits, or an expired card. Please verify your card details and try again, or switch to an alternative payment method.

## Account & Membership

### What do I need to create an account?
You need a valid email address or a Korean mobile number. Alternatively, you can register instantly using Kakao, Naver, or Google single sign-on. All new accounts start at the Standard tier and receive a one-time 3,000 won welcome coupon valid for 30 days.

### How does the loyalty point system work?
Every purchase earns points equal to 1% of the order total (before shipping). Standard members earn 1%, Plus members earn 3%, and VIP members earn 5%. Points are credited 7 days after delivery confirmation and expire 12 months from the date they are earned. 1 point equals 1 won and can be used on any future order with a minimum redemption of 1,000 points.

### What are the membership tiers and their benefits?
- **Standard**: Default tier. Free Economy shipping on orders over 20,000 won. 1% point reward.
- **Plus**: Spend over 200,000 won in a calendar year. Free Economy shipping on all orders. 3% point reward. Birthday month double-point event.
- **VIP**: Spend over 800,000 won in a calendar year. Free Priority shipping on all orders. 5% point reward. Early access to new product launches. Dedicated VIP support line (1588-0002).

Tier status resets on January 1st each year based on the previous year's spending.

### How can I permanently delete my account and personal data?
Navigate to Settings > Privacy > Delete My Account. After confirming with your password or a one-time verification code, we schedule deletion within 7 business days. All personal data, order history, saved addresses, and payment tokens are purged in compliance with Korea's Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA). Unused points and coupons are forfeited upon deletion.

## Products

### How do I know if a product is in stock?
Each product page shows real-time stock status. "In Stock" means the item ships within 1 business day. "Low Stock" means fewer than 5 units remain. "Pre-Order" items have an estimated restock date displayed. "Sold Out" items can be added to a wishlist, and you will be notified by email when they return.

### Does 초록 코퍼레이션 sell its own brand products?
Yes. Our in-house brand, "Cholog Essentials", covers everyday basics such as stationery, desk organizers, and tech accessories. All Cholog Essentials products are designed in Seoul, carry a 2-year warranty, and are eligible for free returns regardless of reason.

### Can I request a product that is not currently in your catalog?
Yes. Use the "Product Request" form at the bottom of the main page. If we receive 50 or more requests for the same product within 30 days, our merchandising team will evaluate sourcing it. You will receive an email update on the status of your request.

### What warranty applies to electronics and appliances?
All electronics and appliances come with a minimum 1-year manufacturer warranty. Cholog Essentials branded electronics carry an extended 2-year warranty. Warranty claims can be submitted through "My Orders > Warranty Claim" and require the original receipt and photos of the issue.

### Is support available in English?
